## Overview
Narendra Modi is the 14th and current Prime Minister of India, serving since 2014. He was born on **September 17, 1950**, in Vadnagar, Gujarat, and holds a Bachelor's degree in Political Science from Delhi University and a Master's degree in Political Science from Gujarat University. Modi has had a long and distinguished political career, serving as Chief Minister of Gujarat from 2001 to 2014 and leading the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) to victory in the 2014 Lok Sabha elections.

## Key Findings
* Narendra Modi was born in **1950** in Vadnagar, Gujarat, to a humble family.
* He holds a Bachelor's degree in Political Science from Delhi University and a Master's degree in Political Science from Gujarat University.
* Modi served as Chief Minister of Gujarat from **2001 to 2014** and led the BJP to victory in the **2014** Lok Sabha elections.
* He has been re-elected as Prime Minister in **2019** and **2024**.
* Modi is a member of the right-wing **Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP)** and the **Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS)**, a Hindu nationalist organization.

## Detailed Analysis
Modi's early life and education played a significant role in shaping his political career. He was an active participant in debates and theater in school and later studied political science at the University of Delhi. After completing his education, Modi joined the BJP and quickly rose through the ranks, becoming the organizing secretary of the party's Gujarat unit in **1987**. As Chief Minister of Gujarat, Modi implemented several reforms and initiatives, including campaigns to improve the state's **transportation and infrastructure**. As Prime Minister, Modi has continued to focus on **economic development** and **good governance**, introducing initiatives such as the **"Make in India" program** and the **"Digital India" initiative**.

## Conclusion
Narendra Modi is a prominent Indian politician who has served as the Prime Minister of India since **2014**. With a long and distinguished political career, Modi has implemented several reforms and initiatives aimed at promoting economic development and good governance. His leadership and vision have had a significant impact on India, and he continues to be a major figure in Indian politics.
